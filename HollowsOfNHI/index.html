"A Disclosure Rogue"

A traditional roguelike in which you enter a secret cover-up complex to expose a UFO conspiracy. Descend through a dark procedural facility filled with secrets, symbols, and non-human intelligences. Find the Archives of Disclosure, escape, and reveal the truth to the world—or die trying. This modern adaptation of a timeless classic adds new graphics, sound, ambience, and music. Available on desktop, mobile, and tablet devices.

Touchscreen and desktop controls

On a touchscreen, swipe to move, pinch to zoom, and drag to pan around the complex. You can also use a virtual D-pad, with one-finger controls for zooming and panning. Both portrait and landscape orientations are supported. Access every command through modal menus or use the keyboard on desktop. Quick-action buttons provide access to common commands, including automatic navigation to the exit.
