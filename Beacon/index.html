Beacon

A small network monitor for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Check your router, internet connection and servers. See what stopped responding, when it happened and how long it lasted.

macOS 14+ · iOS / iPadOS 17+

Outage history

Offline periods, slow connections and individual target failures are saved with their times and duration. Filter the log and export CSV or JSON. Records stay for 90 days by default; change that in Settings.

Latency

Compare response times and packet loss over 15 minutes, an hour or a day. Inspect spikes and failed checks for each target. The last 24 hours are saved across app restarts.

Diagnostics

Copy or save a Markdown report with connection details, recent results and outages. Identifying details are hidden by default. A live route trace shows the responding hops and their reply times.

Checks & alerts

Ping a host, look up a DNS name, test a TCP port or check an HTTP(S) endpoint. Group targets as LAN, WAN or DNS to see which part of the connection failed. Keep a separate target list for each network.

Get notifications when a connection drops or recovers, or send alerts to your own webhook, ntfy, Slack or Discord.

Mac, iPhone & iPad

On Mac, a radar dish, lighthouse or simple status light sits in the menu bar. Green is online, yellow is degraded, red is offline.

On iPhone and iPad, checks run while the app is open and briefly after you leave it. Later background checks depend on iOS scheduling and Background App Refresh.
