A modern take on the original Rogue. Turn-based, permadeath, one Amulet of Yendor to steal.

Windows · macOS · iOS · Android · 2.0 out on Steam

A full 2.0 run, about an hour long.

"Rogue, but better!"

Fight monsters, grab loot, find the Amulet. The dungeon keeps its state and gets stranger the deeper you go. Tiles, ASCII or high contrast.

Touch and desktop controls

Swipe, pinch and drag, or use the D-pad. Rebindable keyboard on desktop; gamepad and Steam Deck in alpha.

Adjustable difficulty

Turn off the harshest old rules, or keep them. Seeded runs, undo and optional hunger.

New in 2.0 – The Dungeon Wakes Up

A livelier dungeon

Things move, monsters snore, hits show damage numbers.

Minimap and a talking hero

The map stays in the corner. Your hero comments on what he sees.

Run records

Depth, kills, gold and turns per run, plus your all-time bests.

Also: faster movement, a detailed combat log option and many fixes.
